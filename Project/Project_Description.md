## Problem/Need
The proposed Courier Parcel Tracking System aims to provide an efficient and automated solution for managing the delivery of packages, mail, and permitted goods from point A to point B. The system will enable real-time parcel tracking, allowing customers and courier staff to monitor delivery progress, improve transparency, reduce delays, and ensure safer and more timely deliveries.

## Users
- Customers (Senders & Recipients) — send and receive packages
- Field Staff — responsible for the actual transportation of the package from sender to receiver
- Administrators — manage user accounts, oversee the centralized database, resolve delivery disputes, and generate shipping labels or waybills

## Main Features
- User registration, authentication, and role-based access control
- Parcel booking with sender, recipient, and parcel information
- Automatic generation of unique tracking numbers
- Real-time parcel status and delivery progress tracking
- Pickup, shipment, and delivery assignment management
- Shipping label and waybill generation with barcode/QR code support
- Delivery confirmation and proof of delivery
- Notifications for parcel status changes, pickups, deliveries, and delays
- Shipping cost calculation and payment-status management
- Management of cancelled, returned, damaged, delayed, or undelivered parcels
- Complaint and delivery dispute management
- Administrative dashboard for monitoring users, parcels, and delivery operations
- Reports and analytics for shipments, deliveries, and staff performance

## Key Functionality
The system maintains a complete digital record of each parcel from booking and pickup through transportation and final delivery. Customers use the tracking number to monitor their shipment, field staff update delivery statuses and provide delivery confirmation, and administrators oversee the entire courier operation through a centralized management system.
