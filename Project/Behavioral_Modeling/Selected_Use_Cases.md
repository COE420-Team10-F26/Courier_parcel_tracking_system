# Selected Use Cases

The team selected four major use cases from the Lab 3 use case model, one per member, for sequence modeling. Together they cover the key stages of a parcel (booking, assignment, delivery and tracking) and cover all three actors. Use case IDs are the team-level IDs from the consolidated use case table in `Project/Requirements/Team_Consolidated_Requirements.md`.

| Use Case ID | Use Case Name | Primary Actor | Purpose | Reason for Selection | Contributor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UC-03 | Book Parcel | Customer | A logged-in customer books a parcel by entering the sender, recipient and parcel details. The system validates them, creates the parcel with a unique tracking number, sets it to Pending Pickup and shows the expected delivery window. | Every parcel in the system starts here. It involves several collaborating objects (Customer, Recipient, Parcel, StatusHistoryEntry, DelayAlert, StatusNotification) and has a clear valid/invalid branch shown with guards. It includes UC-04 Generate Tracking Number. | Sarhan Alzaabi (b00100669) |
| UC-09 | Assign Parcels to Field Staff | Admin | The admin bulk-assigns "Pending Pickup" parcels to one active field staff member. The parcels move to "Assigned", each customer is notified and the staff member gets one summary notification. | It is the core dispatch step that links booking to delivery. It involves many collaborating objects, shows iteration (per staff member, per parcel) and guards (staff active, valid parcels, active delay alert), and includes UC-08 and UC-18. | Kevin Alex (b00098368) |
| UC-16 | Record Proof of Delivery | Field Staff | A field staff member captures the recipient's name, signature and Emirates ID for a parcel that is out for delivery. The parcel is marked "Delivered" only once the proof is complete, and the customer is notified. | It completes the delivery part of the parcel's life and enforces the rule that a parcel can only be marked delivered after proof is captured (FR-15). It includes UC-14 Update Parcel Status and uses guards on the parcel's status and the proof. | Muhammad Hammad Khan (b00094579) |
| UC-19 | View Parcel Tracking History | Customer | A customer enters a tracking number and sees the full, timestamped status history of their own parcel, or a "not found" message. | It reads the history that the other three use cases create, so it ties the set together. It shows iteration over the history entries and an alt fragment that checks whether the parcel is found and belongs to the customer. | Ahmed El Sabagh (b00095506) |

The four use cases are different from one another, each sequence diagram focuses on one use case, and every operation used in the diagrams is already in the Lab 5 class diagram.
