# Project Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest / Expectations | Influence on the System | Impact of System on Stakeholder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customers (Senders) | Book parcels for delivery | Want an easy way to book a parcel and track it without calling anyone | Medium — their booking behavior and expectations shape what the interface needs to support | High — directly relies on the system to know where their parcel is |
| Customers (Recipients) | Receive parcels | Want visibility into when their delivery will arrive | Low — mostly passive, receives notifications | Medium — benefits from tracking but doesn't interact with the system as often |
| Field Staff | Handle pickup and delivery | Need a fast, simple way to update status without slowing down their work | High — if the interface is slow or complicated, they'll skip using it, which breaks the whole tracking system | High — their day to day workflow changes from manual handoffs to logging updates in the app |
| Administrators | Manage accounts and operations | Need visibility into all parcels and users to resolve issues | High — their reporting and monitoring needs shape what the dashboard has to show | High — depends on the system to actually manage operations instead of doing it manually |
